## Rules out / do not repeat

- **Do not repeat:** rolling from a snapshot taken during the game load
  transition (66M+ candidates cannot converge inside the 120s lease; the
  resulting session discard surfaces as a confusing compare `400`).
- **Do not repeat:** trusting a fixed settle window alone — a 25s blind wait
  can still overlap a monitor revocation. Verify steady state explicitly
  (steady replay clock / sane candidate count) before snapshotting.
- Automated CE Windows-debugger write-BPs stay ruled out (OD-020).
